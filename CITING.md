# Citing this workshop and the underlying work

Tutorial maps produced here are not an operational ATES product. If you use
the method or the code after ISSW, cite the papers, not this repository
alone.

## Papers (ISSW 2026)

Sykes, J., Knies, D., Haegeli, P., Anthony-Malone, K., & Statham, G.
(2026). *AutoATES v3.0: Automated ATES mapping at scale across western
Canada.* Proceedings of the International Snow Science Workshop, Whistler,
Canada.

Sykes, J., et al. (2026). *Characterizing differences among automated
avalanche terrain mapping approaches.* Proceedings of the International
Snow Science Workshop, Whistler, Canada. [Connaught Creek multi-agency
comparison]

Knies, D., et al. (2026). Runout calibration paper (ISSW 2026). Use the
final proceedings citation when available.

## Software

- autoATES v3.0 ISSW snapshot: `https://github.com/AutoATES/autoATES-v3.0-issw`
- AvaFrame com4FlowPy: D'Amboise et al.; see AvaFrame documentation and
  the pin in SETUP.md
- ATES v.2 technical model: Statham & Campbell (2025)

## Data

- Elevation: ALOS World 3D – 30 m (AW3D30), JAXA
- Optical imagery: Sentinel-2 Level-2A, Copernicus
- Expert ATES map at Connaught Creek: Sykes et al. (2024) consensus
  mapping, used here as an overlay for discussion

Please respect the licences on those products if you redistribute a
derived map.
